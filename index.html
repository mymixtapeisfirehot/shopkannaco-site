What we do

How the business works.

The company does two things: it buys inventory for less than it can sell for, and it tracks the cost of every unit closely enough to know whether that's actually true.

01 Sourcing Inventory is bought through authorized retail and wholesale channels, logged at the price actually paid.
02 Distribution Resale happens through established online marketplaces and direct sales to individual buyers.
03 Cost tracking An internal system records landed cost and margin per unit, so prices are set from real numbers.
