01 · What we do

Sourcing, then distribution.

The business has two working halves — getting inventory in below market, and getting it back out through the channels that actually net money.

Sourcing

Inventory acquired through authorized retail and wholesale channels, tracked at true landed cost.

Distribution

Resale through established online marketplaces and direct sales to end buyers.

Measured, not guessed

An in-house system tracks cost basis and margin per unit, so pricing is set against real numbers.
