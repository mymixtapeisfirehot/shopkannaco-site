About

Sourcing done properly, sold for what it's worth.

KANNACO LLC is a New Jersey single-member LLC, formed in August 2026. The company sources inventory through retail and wholesale channels and resells it through online marketplaces and direct sales.

Cost discipline is the whole operating model. Every unit is logged at what was actually paid for it, not a rounded estimate, so pricing is set from real numbers rather than a guess at what the market will bear.

Inventory is bought through authorized retail and wholesale channels only. Nothing is listed for sale until it's physically in hand and its condition has been checked.

The company deals in categories where demand at release regularly outpaces available supply, which is what makes disciplined buying and honest pricing worth doing in the first place.

Principles

What this business won't do.

Three rules that don't bend, regardless of what's available or how good a deal looks.

01 No guessed pricing Every price is set from a real, logged cost — never a market estimate dressed up as a fact.
02 No unauthorized sourcing Inventory comes from retail and wholesale channels the business is actually authorized to buy from.
03 No selling what isn't in hand Nothing is listed until it has actually arrived and been checked.
